Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome if we have availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance via WhatsApp to secure your preferred time and barber.

Where are you located?

We are centrally located at C. San Ramón, 4, 22300 Barbastro, in the beautiful province of Huesca, Spain.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday from 9:00 AM - 1:30 PM & 3:30 PM - 9:00 PM, Tuesday through Saturday from 9:30 AM - 1:30 PM & 4:00 PM - 9:00 PM, and Sunday from 9:30 AM - 1:30 PM & 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM.

Do you cut children's hair?

Absolutely. We have a completely patient and specialized approach for kids haircuts to make sure they feel comfortable and look incredibly stylish.

How do you sanitize your tools?

Hygiene is our top priority. We use hospital-grade Barbicide for all our clippers and scissors, switch disposable razor blades for every single client, and implement thorough hand washing between appointments.

What are the standard prices for barber services at Redouane? +

At Barbershop Redouane in Barbastro, we offer premium grooming at highly competitive prices. Our classic Gentleman's Haircut is priced at just 10€. We also offer excellent rates for beard trims, skin fades, and kids' haircuts. Feel free to contact us via WhatsApp to ask about our combo rates!

What additional services do you offer besides haircuts? +

We specialize in complete men's grooming. Beyond classic and modern haircuts, we offer straight razor beard trims, precision lining, eyebrow shaping, and our signature traditional hot towel shave, designed to provide maximum relaxation and excellent skin care.

What is the difference between a barbershop and a hair salon? +

The main difference is specialization. While a hair salon is typically a generalist center for everyone, a barbershop like ours in Barbastro focuses exclusively on men's grooming. Barbers are experts in straight razor work, beard sculpting, and specific masculine techniques like skin fades and classic gentleman’s cuts that require a level of precision often not found in a standard salon.

What are some good questions to ask my barber during an appointment? +

To get the best result, we recommend asking: 1. "Which haircut best suits my face shape?" 2. "How can I maintain this style at home daily?" 3. "Which products (wax or beard oil) do you recommend for my hair type?" At Barbershop Redouane, we love advising our clients so they can keep their sharp look even weeks after their visit.

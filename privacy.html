Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

1. Introduction

Welcome to Barbershop Redouane. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. The Data We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us to book an appointment. Because our bookings are handled directly via WhatsApp, the only personal data we process is:

  • Your name (if provided)
  • Your phone number
  • Any details regarding your appointment request

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Schedule and manage your haircut or grooming appointments.
  • Communicate with you regarding your booking (e.g., confirmations or cancellations).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

5. Your Legal Rights (GDPR)

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of the personal data that we hold about you.
  • Request erasure of your personal data (the right to be forgotten).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly at our shop in Barbastro or via our business WhatsApp at +34 614 11 94 58.
